# Cam & Go Rentals: project documentation (INTERNAL)

> **Keep this repository PRIVATE.** These documents list provisional internal business rules
> (extension fees, overdue fees, deposit policy) that are deliberately *not* published on the customer website.

## Status labels used in every document
| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **[CONFIRMED]** | A working requirement you stated. Structural, unlikely to change, but still open to the owner's feedback. |
| **[PROVISIONAL]** | A working assumption. May change after the business owner reviews the prototype. |
| **[RECOMMENDED]** | A technical suggestion from the developer. Not a business decision. |
| **[DECISION NEEDED]** | Not decided. Needs the owner's approval. Nothing has been invented to fill the gap. |
