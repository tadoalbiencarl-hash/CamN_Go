# Decisions for the business owner

Tick each item after the prototype review. Items marked (P) are values already assumed in the prototype.

## Pricing and policy
- [ ] Are ₱350/day (1-3 days) and ₱300/day (4+ days) final? Does ₱300 apply to *all* days once a rental reaches 4 days? (P)
- [ ] Is inclusive day counting correct (pickup day = Day 1)? Is a same-day rental allowed? (P)
- [ ] Security deposit: none? (P)
- [ ] Extension fee ₱500/day, maximum 2 days? Should it ever be a different rate? (P)
- [ ] Overdue fee ₱1,000/day? Is there a grace period or an hourly rule? (P)
- [ ] What time of day is a camera due back? Can pickup/return happen at any hour?
- [ ] What happens after the maximum extension is reached (call, collect, report)?

## Operations
- [ ] Pickup/return location, hours, and holidays or blackout dates.
- [ ] Cancellation policy and no-show handling. Refunds (cash)? Partial refunds for early return?
- [ ] Accessories included with each camera (the checklist used at handover and return).
- [ ] Damage/loss charges and who decides them. Minimum renter age.
- [ ] Which government IDs are accepted? Must the name on the ID match the application?
- [ ] Receipts: paper, digital, or both? Receipt numbering?
- [ ] How should the admins be notified of overdue rentals (email, SMS, Messenger)?
- [ ] How many camera units, and will more models be added?

## Records and privacy
- [ ] How long are customer records and ID images kept? When are completed transactions archived?
- [ ] Get professional advice on the Data Privacy Act (RA 10173) before collecting real IDs.

## Prototype feedback
- [ ] Does the beige/brown look match the brand? Anything to rename or reword?
- [ ] Which dashboard screens matter most? Anything missing?
