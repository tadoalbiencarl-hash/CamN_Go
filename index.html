Frequently asked questions

Does submitting an application reserve the camera?

No. Applications are reviewed first. A rental is only confirmed after we contact you.

What do I need to rent?

A completed application and a valid ID. See the policies page for details.

How is the price computed?

₱350 per day for 1–3 days; ₱300 per day when you rent for 4 days or more. Days are counted by calendar date, and your pickup day counts as Day 1 (for example, Monday to Wednesday is 3 days).

Are the dates on the calendar real?

Not yet. This prototype uses sample availability data only.
